# Audit: deterministic-field discontinuities at ISB/RSB and inside the shadow

Date: 2026-07-16. Scope: single-cube PEC full-wave validation case
(`single_cube-metal`, 5 GHz, receiver plane z=0.10 m cutting the cube,
recorded run `.test-tmp/fullwave-smoke/visual-deterministic-metal-centered-5ghz-256.npz`).

## Summary

The UTD coefficient core is mathematically correct: at ISB/RSB toggle pairs the
measured diffraction step matches the GO step at median ratio 1.02 (ISB) and
0.998 (RSB) with near-zero median phase error. The discontinuities are caused
by hard **gating and export defects around the coefficient**, not by the
Kouyoumjian–Pathak math itself.

## Data evidence

Per-component decomposition of the recorded field (`los + reflection +
diffraction + transmission == field` verified to 1.8e-9):

- Adjacent-cell |E| jumps: median 0.085 dB, p99 3.4 dB, max 156.9 dB.
- Of 1544 pairs jumping > 3 dB, only 74 lie on a LoS/reflection support
  toggle (ISB/RSB). 1472 lie in the *interior* of component support — the
  diffraction field itself is discontinuous.
- The diffraction magnitude map is a patchwork of polygonal blocks with
  straight seams along the extended cube-face planes (x=±0.1, y=±0.1) and
  along rays from TX through cube vertices.
- Dead sliver next to the +x face (x∈[0.103,0.122], y∈[-0.091,-0.059]):
  |E_diff| ~ 1e-10 next to cells at ~1e-3..7.9e-3 → 100–157 dB jumps.
- Fake interference nulls on the lit side of the vertex-generated ISB
  (clusters at (0,0.68) and (0.36,0.63)): |E_diff| ≈ |E_los| in antiphase
  (should be ≤ E_i/2 for single-edge UTD), then drops 3x in one cell.

## Root causes (ranked)

### R1 — `UTD_MIN_DISTANCE = 5e-2` hard zeroing (PROVEN)

`rayd/shared/utd/utd_math.h:1161` (`compute_pair_field_terms`) and the vector
twin at `:1317`:

```cpp
geomValid = srcExt && (sP > UTD_MIN_DISTANCE) && (s > UTD_MIN_DISTANCE);
if (!geomValid) return;   // UTD_MIN_DISTANCE = 5.0e-2f (utd_types.h:29)
```

Any receiver within 5 cm of the Keller point gets a hard zero for that edge.
The receiver plane cuts the cube, so near-edge receivers are guaranteed.
Verification: all 15/15 dead-sliver cells (256-grid) have stationary-point
distance s ∈ [0.0117, 0.0484] m < 0.05 to the SE vertical edge; 0/48 nearby
live shadow cells contradict. 5 cm ≈ 0.83 λ at 5 GHz.

### R2 — scalar export collapses vectors to "total amplitude × dominant-component phase"

`native/channel_native/kernels/deterministic_field.cu:225-256`
(`collapse_field_vector`) and `:333-379`
(`deterministic_diffraction_vector_field_kernel`): each *path's* complex
3-vector is collapsed to a scalar carrying the **argmax Cartesian
component's phase** rescaled to total amplitude; the coherent accumulator
sums these per-path scalars. Phase jumps whenever the argmax switches;
multi-path interference is computed in an inconsistent scalar space; the
exported "field" is not a fixed vector component (Maxwell-Ez comparison
semantically broken, recorded complex coherence 2e-5).

### R3 — hard receiver-side branch switches inside the diffraction evaluation

- **a. `endpointContinuation = selectedStationary && !tgtExt`**
  (`utd_math.h:1171-1176`): switches coefficient formula when the receiver
  crosses an *infinite* face plane (`wedge_exterior_mask`).
- **b. binary two-segment occlusion of the diffracted ray**
  (`paths_optix.cu:443-446`, `kDfrRayBias=1e-4`): grazing rays from the
  on-edge Q along the wedge's own faces toggle chaotically.
- **c. Keller stationary point clamped to the endpoint**
  (`paths_optix.cu:441`) + ad-hoc `exp(-u²)` taper
  (`finite_wedge_stationary_completion_factor`, `utd_math.h:1102-1134`):
  non-C0 handoff, wrong shape (1.0 inside the edge instead of →1/2 at the
  endpoint).

### R4 — no vertex (corner) diffraction; overlapping edge transition regions double-count

Order-1 edge UTD only. Near a shadow boundary generated by a cube *vertex*
the adjacent edges' transition regions overlap and each contributes
≈ E_i/2 with the completion factor pinned at 1.0 → fake nulls; one cell
over, one contribution drops discontinuously.

### R5 — inconsistent polarization models across components

- LoS: isotropic scalar (no polarization) — `deterministic_field.cu:311-331`.
- Reflection: `initial_transverse_polarization` (deterministic_field.cu:169-178)
  hard-codes TX pol (0,0,1), **normalizes the transverse projection**
  (destroying the sin θ pattern weight), and substitutes an arbitrary unit
  vector at the axial null.
- Diffraction: true TX polarization with full Jones transport, then
  collapsed per R2.

### R6 — minor contributors

4-sample binary TX edge prefilter; float32 `cot·F` noise near boundaries
(bounded); the `roundf` N± selection is continuous by construction
(verified, not a defect); reflection EPC in-face containment and LoS
visibility are hard toggles by design (GO) — fine given a correct
compensating UTD term.

## Addendum (2026-07-17): per-path instrumentation results

Phase-C instrumented verification (per-path dumps at the anomaly cells,
fresh build fp fc17ede8) refined the attribution:

- **R1 CONFIRMED at scale**: on a fine near-face patch, 582/798 receivers
  east of the +x face lose the SE-edge contribution exactly when
  min(s,s') < 0.05 (geom_valid=0, stationary point inside the edge, tgt
  exterior). The alive/dead boundary sits at s = 0.0516/0.0499.
- **R4 CONFIRMED, worse than assumed**: at the NW-top vertex ray, THREE
  edges (NW-vertical, N-top, W-top) all carry stationary points clamped
  to/past the vertex and sum to |E_diff,z| ≈ |E_los| in antiphase (fake
  null on the fully lit side). Correct corner limit ≈ E_los/2.
- **R3c CONFIRMED as the vertex-seam driver**: the one-cell 11 dB drop is
  the parameter-exit handoff (N-top t* re-enters [0,L]) plus the completion
  factor's raw/boundary ratio flipping sign for NW-vert. No path
  appears/disappears there (R3b refuted at that particular seam).
- **R3b CONFIRMED at the NE-vertex ISB**: N-top's segments graze the north
  face within the 1e-4 bias zone and toggle occlusion (14.4 dB jump).
- **R2 CONFIRMED spectacularly** at the dip-north anomaly: two edges'
  fields live in ORTHOGONAL Cartesian components (z vs y); the
  dominant-component collapse assigns them opposite scalar phases and
  fabricates a 20 dB null (the physical vector field varies only 3 dB
  there).
- **ISB proper is continuous in the vector domain**: crossing the LoS
  toggle, the physical |total_z| step is 0.8% of the LoS step. The R5
  isotropic-LoS error is a smooth ~4% (sin θ, θ≈74°) offset. ISB-tail
  excess is therefore mostly geometry gates (R3b/R3c/R4), not R2/R5.
- **Attribution correction**: the lit-region diffraction-component seams on
  the y=-0.4031 line sit exactly on φ+φ′=π (RSB0) / φ+φ′=2π (RSBn)
  crossings where the GO reflection toggles simultaneously — those
  component steps are BY DESIGN (they compensate the reflection toggle);
  boundary registration there is correct. The endpointContinuation branch
  (R3a) was NOT observed to fire at any instrumented seam; it remains the
  only explanation for the full-length |x|=0.1 / |y|=0.1 seams in the
  diffraction map (horizontal top edges whose infinite-wedge interior
  covers half the domain).

Additional findings: the path solver already exports a
receiver-polarization projection (`field_project_complex3`) — the model
for the deterministic R2 fix. BDPT diffraction is not UTD at all
(bdpt_connect_common.cuh:226 — 1/r²·1/r² heuristic with a clamped
wedge-angle scale); fixing R1–R6 does not make it physical — documented as
separate future work. MC-basic same-family extras: φ>exterior hard reject
(diffraction.cu:341); reflection blocker binary occlusion with 1e-4 bias
and no self-face handling (reflection.cu:177).
